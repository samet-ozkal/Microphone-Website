Introducing the LCT 440 Pure

Condenser Microphone

Experience pure studio longevity – Finally capture 100% studio quality footage with 0% processing effort. Ready to record right away for singing, language, instrument recordings, podcasting and live broadcasting. Crystal clear sound thanks to the 1” true condenser capsule. Perfect for studio and home recording – Noise behind the mic is suppressed by -20dB thanks to the kidney feature, keeping your voice in focus. Built-in microphone spider noise is minimized and pop protection reduces pops by 15dB for pure studio sound.
